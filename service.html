Service

I am the president of the Texas A&M Cybersecurity Club. As cybersecurity is a broad field, I help students discover different areas of the industry and where they feel like they fit in. Additionally, I actively engage in establishing connections with recruiters and inviting distinguished guest speakers to our club's events. Through these networking opportunities, our members gain insights and access to valuable resources within the industry. Furthermore, I am responsible for organizing and scheduling our club meetings and activity groups. This club helped me discover not only my technical interests--but myself. Because of that, I am devoted to giving back to the club in any way I can.
