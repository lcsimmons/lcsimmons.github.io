Qualifications

Internships

In summer 2022, I interned with Sandia National Labs. I assisted the incident response team with programming automated threat detection engineering pipelines. There, I also worked on creating an incident response training program; I developed attack patterns based on the MITRE ATT&CK framework and configured forensic systems to create artifacts for a simulated cybersecurity attack.

However, this past summer, I worked at Battelle as a vulnerability research and reverse engineering intern. I had an incredibly fulfilling experience--and even got to learn ARM! I am excited to return next summer.

Awards

Twice, I have been named a National Cyber Scholar by the National Cyber Scholarship Foundation. I first won this award in high school for being a top-scorer in the National Cyber Scholarship CTF. I was one of 68 high school students in Texas to receive this award. The second time I won this award, I was a freshman in college. I earned it through completing a high number of cybersecurity challenges on the Cyberstart America platform.
